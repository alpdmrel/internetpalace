Voice control is excellent until you are half asleep, and a phone is worse — it requires
unlocking a bright screen to turn a light off. This is the counterweight: a small panel at the
bedside with a fixed button for each of the few actions that are worth a dedicated control.

Beam light, desk lamp, window light, turn off the PC, goodmorning, goodnight. Nothing
configurable, nothing that moves — the value is entirely in the buttons always being in the
same place in the dark.

## Why it is on hold

It overlaps heavily with the [bed sensor](/projects/bed-sensor), which triggers goodnight and
goodmorning automatically from occupancy. Once that works well, half of this panel's buttons
are solving a problem that no longer exists. Parked pending a decision on what is actually
left for it to do.
